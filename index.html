🌿 Vikruti · Imbalance

📜 Charaka Samhita

"Ahara sambhavam vastu rogah sarve api..."
All diseases arise from improper food; all health from proper nourishment. Food is the supreme medicine.
— Charaka Samhita · Nidana Sthana 6.4
"Na kinchidaushudham loke yadabeshajam bhavet..."
There is nothing in this world that cannot be used as medicine when applied with wisdom.
— Charaka Samhita · Sutra Sthana 26.12

⚗️ The Three Doshas

Vata · Air + Ether
Balanced: Creative, vibrant, quick mind.
Excess: Anxiety, dry skin, bloating, insomnia.
Balance with: Warm, oily, sweet · Oil massage · Routine
Pitta · Fire + Water
Balanced: Sharp intellect, strong digestion, leadership.
Excess: Inflammation, acid, anger, rashes.
Balance with: Cool, bitter, sweet · Avoid spicy · Evening walks
Kapha · Earth + Water
Balanced: Strong immunity, calm endurance, deep love.
Excess: Weight gain, lethargy, congestion, fog.
Balance with: Light, dry, warm, spicy · Vigorous exercise · Early rising

🌿 Daily Herbs

💊 Rasayana

⭐
Triphala — Supreme Rasayana
Amalaki+Bibhitaki+Haritaki. Tridoshic. ½ tsp warm water at bedtime. Balances all doshas, detoxifies, supports weight loss. The single most important Ayurvedic supplement.
🌿
Ashwagandha
Reduces cortisol, builds Ojas and immunity, supports muscle tone, reduces stress-eating. Warm milk at night.
🍀
Guduchi (Giloy) — Amrita
Tridoshic Rasayana. Excellent Pitta pacifier, immune booster, reduces Ama, supports liver. Daily juice or powder.
🍂
Guggulu
Charaka's Medohara Guggulu for obesity (Sthaulya). Reduces fat tissue and Ama powerfully. Anti-inflammatory.
🫚
Fenugreek (Methi)
Reduces Kapha, regulates blood sugar, kindles Agni. Soak overnight, chew on empty stomach. Use liberally in cooking.
Shadrasas · Six Tastes
Charaka teaches that a complete meal includes all six tastes in proportions right for your dosha.
🟢
Madhura (Sweet)
Increases Kapha, decreases Vata & Pitta. Rice, wheat, milk, ghee, sweet fruits. Use sparingly for Kapha.
Charaka · Sutra 26.40
🔴
Amla (Sour)
Increases Pitta & Kapha. Lemon, tamarind, amla. Pitta types: very sparingly.
Charaka · Sutra 26.43
🔵
Lavana (Salty)
Use Sendha Namak (rock salt) only. Minimal amounts. Increases Pitta & Kapha.
Charaka · Sutra 26.46
🟠
Katu (Pungent)
Decreases Kapha. Ginger, pepper — moderate amounts. Avoid excess chili (aggravates Pitta).
Charaka · Sutra 26.49
🟤
Tikta (Bitter) — Your Best Taste
Decreases Pitta & Kapha. Bitter gourd, neem, methi, turmeric. EXCELLENT — use liberally.
Charaka · Sutra 26.52
⚪
Kashaya (Astringent)
Decreases Pitta & Kapha. Pomegranate, lentils, green tea. Excellent — reduces inflammation.
Charaka · Sutra 26.55

📖 Ahara Vidhi

01
Eat only when truly hungry
Only when previous meal is fully digested. Signs: lightness, proper hunger, clear belch.
Charaka · Sutra 8.25
Critical
02
Eat warm, freshly cooked food
Warm food kindles Agni. Cold dampens Agni and increases Kapha. Stale food is Tamasic.
Charaka · Sutra 5.7
Important
03
Fill stomach ½ — proper measure
½ food, ¼ water, ¼ empty. "Atiyogah sarvarogakaranam" — overeating is the root of all disease.
Charaka · Sutra 5.3
Critical
04
Eat in a peaceful, grateful state
Never eat when angry, anxious or distracted. Mental state directly affects digestive fire.
Charaka · Vimana 1.24
Important
05
Avoid Viruddha Ahara
Never: honey+hot water · fruit+grain · curd at night · fish+milk. These create deep Ama.
Charaka · Sutra 26.83
Critical
06
Chew 32 times per bite
Digestion begins in the mouth. Bodhaka Kapha prepares food for downstream digestion.
Charaka · Sutra 8.29
Tip
"Vyayamad labhathe swasthyam, laghavam, karma samarthata..."
From exercise: lightness, ability to work, firmness, tolerance, stimulation of digestive fire. Exercise at half-capacity only.
— Charaka Samhita · Sutra Sthana 7.31

Virechana (Purgation)
Primary Pitta treatment. Seasonal cleanse in autumn. Clears Pitta from small intestine and liver.

Vamana (Emesis)
Primary Kapha treatment. Best in spring. Effective for obesity and congestion. Under guidance.
💨
Basti (Medicated Enema)
"Half of all therapies" — Charaka. Primary Vata treatment. Medicated oil or herbal decoctions.
✨
Nasya (Nasal Therapy)
Anu Taila — 2–3 drops each nostril in morning. Clears Kapha from head. Improves sense organs.
